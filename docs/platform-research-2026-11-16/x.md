# X (Twitter) API v2 - capabilities research for GeekSocial

Researched 2026-11-16 from official docs only (docs.x.com raw `.md` pages, fetched directly). Legend: CONFIRMED = read in an official page I opened (quote + URL). UNVERIFIED = docs silent, conflicting, or page not openable. BLOCKED = unsupported, closed, or needs approval/tier we do not have.

Dating note: the pricing page itself carries NO date. Dating evidence from the official changelog (https://docs.x.com/changelog.md, newest entry 2026-11-10): pay-per-use launched 2026-02-06; "Owned Reads" and new write prices effective 2026-04-20; self-serve reply restriction 2026-02-23; free-credit incentives 2026-10-02. Developer Agreement/PPU Agreement header: "Last Updated: April 27, 2026".

Headline findings (read these first):
1. X has NO Free/Basic/Pro price list in current docs. The only documented self-serve model is pay-per-usage credits. Basic/Pro "remain available" per one changelog line but their prices/caps are not in any page I could open.
2. Non-polling delivery of mentions and replies IS documented below Enterprise: X Activity API (XAA) `post.mention.create` and `post.reply.create` over a webhook, billed per event, 1,500 subscriptions on self-serve.
3. BLOCKED-style risks for our exact product: (a) API replies by self-serve apps are only permitted if the original post's author "summoned" the replier; (b) the Developer Guidelines say "AI-powered app generates and posts replies - Requires prior approval from X"; (c) the Agreement says self-serve plans are for "a limited number of end-users" and anything beyond needs Enterprise.
4. Reading a customer's mentions is NOT eligible for the cheap $0.001 "Owned Read" price (requires the authenticated user to own the developer app). It is $0.005 per post.
5. Refresh tokens: single-use and about 6 months (stated on the OAuth 1.0a migration page). Access token 2 hours.

Plan inclusion is inferred from (a) a "Self-serve" row in the XAA subscription-limit table and (b) per-event webhook prices on the pay-per-use pricing page; there is no single sentence "XAA is included in pay-per-use". ## 9. Open items (need a live call or a sales/policy conversation)

1. Written approval from X for AI-generated replies (Policy Support form). Unknown: per app or per customer account, and what evidence they require. Until granted: BLOCKED for any auto-send. Drafts a human approves are still "AI-powered app generates and posts replies" under the quoted row, so they are arguably covered too. Ask.
2. Enterprise requirement: the "limited number of end-users" clause. Get X to state the end-user threshold and Enterprise price for a multi-tenant app. Enterprise pricing is UNVERIFIED.
3. "Summoned" semantics: does a commenter's direct reply to the customer's post (which carries an @mention of the customer in its text) allow our app to reply under the self-serve rule? Needs a live POST /2/tweets test against a real reply, and whether the cheaper "Post: Create (summoned)" $0.01 price is applied.
4. Replying to mentions from users who are not replying to the customer's post (only an @mention): same live test. Also thread posts (replying to own post): does the summoned rule apply?
5. Reply read gaps: `post.reply.create` is direct replies only; replies-to-replies need an on-demand search by conversation_id (7-day window).
6. XAA on pay-per-use: confirm with a live subscription creation: (a) allowed on a pay-per-use app, (b) number of webhooks allowed, (c) whether delivered events count toward the 3M monthly read cap, (d) whether `post.mention.create` / `post.reply.create` bill $0.005 (pricing table lists only `post.create`), (e) whether the subscription survives access-token expiry (docs say it persists until deleted or the user revokes).
7. Webhook retry policy for failed deliveries is not documented; only the 24-hour replay endpoint. CRC cadence conflict (30 minutes vs hourly).
8. Basic/Pro prices and caps, and the legacy Free tier: https://developer.x.com/#pricing was not fetchable (HTTP 402). Check in the Developer Console (console.x.com).
9. Media upload price (not in tables); alt-text scope (images only?); whether media upload counts in any write budget.
10. Authorize URL: x.com/i/oauth2/authorize vs api.x.com/2/oauth2/authorize.
11. The refresh-token 6-month figure comes from the OAuth 1.0a migration guide; confirm on a live refresh token. Confirm the exact `expires_in` returned.
12. Deletion compliance without a timer: batch compliance jobs and compliance streams (Enterprise) are the documented mechanisms; the tier for batch compliance is not stated. Design question against the no-polling rule.
13. Whether a customer's brand account that sends AI/auto replies must display the "Automated" profile label and a bio disclosure (rules are written for bot accounts; the Automation Rules page itself could not be opened).
14. Whether the 3M monthly Post-read cap is per app or per billing account: post-cap.md says usage is "tracked at the app level"; the pricing page says per "monthly billing cycle".
